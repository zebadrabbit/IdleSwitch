# Wyes Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Track that leaves the horizontal: a seeded wye per terminal, first as a Keddie-style junction with a third portal (Phase 1), then as a Tampa-style stub terminus with reversing moves (Phase 2), on a direction model that takes travel from the path instead of from east/west.

**Architecture:** Two refactor tasks land the new direction model on the existing geometry with byte-identical behaviour (fixtures prove it), then the generator grows the junction, the sim grows three-portal traffic and branch traffic locking, and the renderer places signals and tags by facing. Phase 2 adds the stub geometry, `reverse()`, shunt signals and the terminus mission. Pure modules stay pure; `ui.js` barely changes.

**Tech Stack:** Vanilla ES modules, Canvas 2D; `node:assert` tests via `./manage.sh test` and CI.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-02-wyes-design.md` (binding; read the sections named in each task)

## Global Constraints

- Pure modules (`gen.js`, `sim.js`, `camera.js`, `ui-util.js`, `rng.js`) never touch window/document/Math.random/Date/performance.
- Same seed, same layout, same day at the same density and day length. Layouts without a wye keep identical node, edge, switch and track lists (fixtures), and the sim on them runs identically (log fixture) through Tasks 1–2; from Task 3 on, non-wye seeds may differ only through `sim.rng` draws the spec adds (the exit coin), and that difference is documented in the task.
- No crossings, no diamonds, ≤ 3 edges per node, switches = exactly the 3-edge nodes.
- Existing invariants in every soak: no double occupancy, no passed red (a train's head never passes a signal at STOP), no STALL, no section with trains of both main directions, at most one non-exempt wrong-main train, queues ≤ `QUEUE_MAX`.
- The round's visual rule still applies: no hard cuts; new drawing goes through the renderer's existing easing.
- Exact values are in the spec: `GAP_WYE = 22`, `GAP_TERM = 35`, legs `wye` 30 mph, stem `branch` 45 mph, stub `platform` 7 cells capacity 6.5, `BRANCH_SHARE = 0.3`, wye probability `0.6`, `PUSH_MPH = 15`, `DWELL.terminus = 600`, three-portal mean interval `DENSITY × 1.5`.
- Commit trailer `Co-Authored-By: Claude Fable 5.1 <noreply@anthropic.com>` after a blank line. `./manage.sh test` green before every commit. Ponytail: shortest diff that works; `// ponytail:` on deliberate ceilings.

## Review Focus

1. A route whose exit node is a dead end that is not an exit portal (the stub buffer): `aspectOf` must treat it as CLEAR-beyond like an exit, and `moveTrains` must stop the train `FOUL` short, never despawn it. Test: Task 7 (back-in stops at the buffer; train still on the board).
2. The branch portal with both directions queued and the stem held inbound by a standing arrival: the second arrival must still be admitted (same sense) and a departure refused; neither may stall past `STALL`. Test: Task 4 opposing/sense tests and the wye soak.
3. A human NX route from `b` through the whole junction into the branch while a train stands at the stem signal: refused `opposing`, red flash, no partial lock left behind. Test: Task 4 "refused route locks nothing".
4. `reverse()` while another train's route lock covers edges behind the reversing train (should be impossible by the atomic-route argument): assert in the soak that at every reversal the run edge and the junction edges are free of other locks. Test: Task 7 soak invariant.
5. Window resize / camera follow while a train climbs the stem: the head point is continuous and the tag stays on the right of travel through the 45° turn. Test: Task 5 `trainBlocks` continuity across the leg and `tagSide` helper tests; visual check.
